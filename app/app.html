Settings

Appearance

Light or dark
Accent

The accent is the colour of everything the app does — links, the current note, a filled progress bar. It is not the colour of your subjects, which you choose per subject.

Reminders

A local notification on this device only — nothing is sent anywhere else to make it happen.

Privacy

Which screens get opened and how often — counts and fixed labels only. It goes to Nexley's own database and nowhere else, there is no advertising or tracking company involved, and it is built so it cannot carry a note, a title or anything you have written. Turning this off changes nothing about how the app works. What is collected.

Your work

A snapshot is taken automatically once a day. Export is a plain file that needs no account and no internet to read back.

Getting help

Feedback comes back to you with a reply. A problem report is write-only on purpose — nothing in it can ever be read back out, which is what keeps your notes out of a crash log.

Account

Removes your account and everything in it — notes, marks, papers, shares and comps. There is no snapshot for this one; it cannot be undone.

Delete your account?

This permanently removes your account and everything in it — every subject, note, mark, paper, share and comp entry. It cannot be undone: there is no snapshot to restore from, because the account itself is gone, not just marked as removed.

Your username

This is how someone sends you a note or invites you to a comp. Pick something you would say out loud — it is not your email, and it is not your real name unless you want it to be.

3 to 20 characters. Letters, numbers and underscores, starting with a letter. You can change it later — anything already sent keeps the name it was sent under.
